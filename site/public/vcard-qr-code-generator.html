vCard QR code generator (contact card)

A contact card QR code stores your details in the standard vCard format. When someone scans it, their phone offers to save your contact, so your name, number, email, company and website are added without typing.

Create your QR code freeSee pricing

Fields you can include

  • First and last name
  • Phone and email
  • Company and job title
  • Website and address

Where to print it

Business cards, email signatures, stall banners, conference badges and shop windows.

Steps

  1. Open the generator and choose Contact card.
  2. Fill in your details.
  3. Download the code. Keep the printed size at least 2.5 cm so phones read it quickly.

Frequently asked questions

Does it expire?

No. The details are inside the code, so it works forever. To change details later, make a new code.

Can I add my logo?

Yes, on Starter and higher plans you can add a logo in the centre.
